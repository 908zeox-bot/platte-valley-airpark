⛽ Fuel Activation Rate

Platte Valley Airpark (18V) · Nov 2025 – Mar 2026 · Source: FuelMaster (Syntech) + FlightAware AeroAPI

📊 Key Findings
🔴Per-landing activation rate declining: 35% in Nov → 26% in Mar 2026 as arrival counts grew. More aircraft visiting, but proportionally fewer fueling per landing.
🟡Unique tail activation strong in winter: 94–107% of tracked unique arrivals also appear as unique fuelers in Nov/Dec — based aircraft fuel frequently. Rate softened to ~75–89% in Jan–Mar as transient traffic increased.
🟢Peak gallons month: Nov 2025 — 1,680 gal / 81 transactions / ~$9,997 est. revenue. Lowest: Feb 2026 at 1,029 gal / 56 transactions.
💡Opportunity: March had the highest arrival count (298) but only 77 fuel transactions (26%). If activation rate recovered to the Nov level (35%), that would be ~104 transactions — roughly 27 additional fueling events per month.
