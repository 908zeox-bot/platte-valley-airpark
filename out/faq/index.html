⛽ Fuel & Services

Does 18V have self-serve fuel?
Yes. Platte Valley Airpark has a self-serve 100LL fuel pump available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Payment is by credit card — no attendant required.
What is the current 100LL fuel price at 18V?
$6.65 per gallon. Self-serve, 24/7, credit card at the pump. Consistently lower than nearby towered airports (KBJC, KAPA, KGXY). Check plattevalleyairpark.com for the latest price.
Is there a landing fee at Platte Valley Airpark?
No. There is no landing fee at 18V. You're welcome to land, fuel up, and go.
What fuel types are available?
100LL avgas only. No Jet-A on field.

📻 Radio & Procedures

What is the CTAF at Platte Valley Airpark (18V)?
122.9 MHz. Platte Valley Airpark is an uncontrolled airport — no tower. Make standard position announcements on 122.9 when operating in the traffic pattern.
What is the traffic pattern altitude at 18V?
5,500 ft MSL (approximately 566 ft AGL above the field elevation of 4,934 ft MSL). Left traffic for Runway 15/33. Check the Chart Supplement and NOTAMs for current procedures.
Is 18V a towered or uncontrolled airport?
Uncontrolled. No tower, no Class D airspace. Self-announce on CTAF 122.9. Airspace is Class G/E at the field.
What is the field elevation at 18V?
4,934 ft MSL. Density altitude is a real factor in summer — plan your performance accordingly. A standard summer day can push density altitude 2,000–3,000 ft above field elevation.

🛬 Runways

What are the runways at 18V?
Two runways: (1) Runway 15/33 — 4,100 ft paved asphalt, main runway; (2) Runway 9/27 — 2,500 ft grass strip, ideal for taildraggers and backcountry-capable aircraft.
Can taildraggers land at Platte Valley Airpark?
Absolutely. The 2,500-foot grass strip (Runway 9/27) is popular with tailwheel and backcountry aircraft. It's a regular training ground for pilots building grass-strip time.
Is the grass runway open year-round?
Generally yes, but condition varies with weather. Check NOTAMs before flying. The 4,100 ft paved runway (15/33) is available year-round.
Are the runways lighted?
Runway 15/33 has pilot-controlled lighting (PCL) on CTAF 122.9 — click the mic 7 times within 5 seconds for high intensity. The grass strip (9/27) is not lighted.

📍 Location & Getting Here

Where is Platte Valley Airpark?
7507 County Road 39, Fort Lupton, Colorado 80621. On Colorado's Front Range, approximately 40 minutes north of Denver. FAA identifier: 18V. Coordinates: 40°6.2′N / 104°42.1′W.
How far is 18V from Denver?
Approximately 35–40 miles north of Denver (KDEN). About 20 miles from Rocky Mountain Metropolitan Airport (KBJC) and about 40 miles from Centennial Airport (KAPA).
How do I get driving directions?
Navigate to 7507 County Road 39, Fort Lupton, CO 80621. Or use Google Maps directions.

🏠 The Hangar House

What is the Hangar House?
The Hangar House is an aviation-themed Airbnb located right on the airport property at Platte Valley Airpark. It's a fully refurbished 1,704 sq ft ranch-style home (built 1942) with direct access to the runways.
How many guests does the Hangar House sleep?
Up to 5 guests. The home has 2 bedrooms (1 king bed + bunk bed with 2 twins) and a living room couch. 2.5 bathrooms, full kitchen with 2 ovens, WiFi, Roku, fireplace, and washer/dryer.
Is aircraft tie-down included with the Hangar House?
Yes. Aircraft tie-down on the field is included with your stay. Temporary hangar space may also be available — contact erin@plattevalleyairpark.com.
How do I book the Hangar House?
The Hangar House is listed on Airbnb. Visit plattevalleyairpark.com/hangar-house for the booking link and photo gallery.
Is the Hangar House pet-friendly?
Yes — dogs on a leash are welcome.

✈️ Hangars & Basing

Can I base my aircraft at Platte Valley Airpark?
Yes. 18V is home to 100+ based aircraft. T-hangars and tie-downs are available. Space is limited — join the waitlist at plattevalleyairpark.com/hangars.
How do I get on the hangar waitlist?
Fill out the waitlist form at plattevalleyairpark.com/hangars or email erin@plattevalleyairpark.com. We'll contact you when space opens up.
Are tie-downs available for transient aircraft?
Yes. Transient tie-downs are available at no charge.
